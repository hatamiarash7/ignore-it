# Context

Teams use different metric names and instrumentation libraries, making service health difficult to compare across the platform.

# Decision

Services will instrument request and runtime metrics with OpenTelemetry and export them through the shared collector to Prometheus.

# Consequences

Dashboards and alerts can use a consistent metric model. Instrumentation and cardinality guidelines are needed to control telemetry volume.
