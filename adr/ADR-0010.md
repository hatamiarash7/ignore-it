# Context

Internal service calls traverse shared network segments. Existing transport encryption is not consistently configured, and services do not verify peer identity.

# Decision

Service-to-service connections will use mutual TLS with certificates issued and rotated by the platform certificate manager.

# Consequences

Traffic is encrypted and callers can authenticate each other. Certificate issuance, rotation, and trust configuration become critical operational capabilities.
